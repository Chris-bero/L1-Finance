Learning Modules

Click on the modules below to explore the core concepts of Corporate Finance.

Module 1: The 5 Principles of Finance +

Finance is the management of money. Whether at the personal, corporate, or public level, it relies on five main principles:

1. Time Value of Money

A dollar received today is better than a dollar received in the future.

2. Risk-Return Trade-off

Higher risk requires the expectation of higher return.

3. Cash Flows are the Source of Value

The amount of cash that can be taken out of the business determines its true value.

4. Market Prices Reflect Information

Buy & Sell decisions are based on available market data and information.

5. Individuals Respond to Incentives

Motivation drives financial and corporate behavior.

Module 3: Capital Management & Sources of Money +

Goal of Capital Management: Provide sufficient liquidity in the short-term to carry out continuing operations.


Sources of Money:

  • Debt: Loans, Debt Securities (Increases default risk if overused)
  • Equity: Preferred stocks, Common stocks (Decreases EPS if overused)

Areas of Corporate Finance:

  • Capital Budgeting: Project selection and long-term investments.
  • Capital Financing: Funding projects/operations via Debt or Equity.
  • Working Capital Management: Managing Current Assets & Current Liabilities.
